## Hard command invariants and representable cap

Nominal cap .15Nm retained, not a per-axis cap. Reject nonfinite/out-of-domain input. Build intended world direction and bounded double vector, explicitly verify whole-vector norm<=cap. The pure prototype uses a Number.EPSILON relative normalization headroom (1-Number.EPSILON) and final exact comparison; this is deterministic arithmetic prevention, NOT an engine error tolerance or gain change. Encode each component to FLOAT32 TOWARD ZERO: nearest=Math.fround(abs(component)); if nearest>abs(component), take its immediate float32 predecessor; restore sign, canonicalize zero. No iterative fit. Each encoded component magnitude<=requested component, hence vector norm cannot grow. Exact dyadic squared-norm comparison against the represented cap verifies the result without cap+epsilon. Record intended/requested/encoded/readback vectors and deltas. Pure encoding tests are NOT evidence that the future engine harness has applied it.

The .15Nm axial encoded torque is .14999999105930328Nm. Old #72 .15000000596046448Nm nearest-round readback stays in raw data with its representation issue; never rewrite that record or pass it through this new cap policy. Future actual accumulator cap must hold strictly and encoding must be logged.

Partner receives EXACT negative of encoded torso WORLD vector (zero signs equivalent). Public resetTorques(true) on both, readback zero, then addTorque(encodedTau,true) once on torso and partner in the SAME PRE-to-POST phase; record a phase log with world step index, both API calls and intervening-step count0. addTorque uses Nm, no dt multiplier. Readbacks must equal the encoded float32 vectors exactly componentwise and have norm<=.15; known userForce must remain zero, all expected bodies dynamic and explicit environment features off. After one world.step, clear both and verify zero. Unexpected prior accumulation, extra call/step, changed body, force/motor/contact or failed cap -> fail_command/invalid_setup. Public getters/audit cannot prove absence of every unobservable engine moment.

Impulse cases: construct physical tau*T once (Nm*s), encode toward zero with capImpulse=.15*T and applyTorqueImpulse once +J/-J in the same phase before any step. userTorque remains zero; immediate angular/linear response recorded. Log actual T used to form impulse, no double multiplication. Impulse API/finite inertia/frames can be validated independently of a continuous engine precision envelope. Keep original immediate-response1e-5rad/s and inverse-tensor-relative1e-5 control markers unchanged as **local diagnostic controls**, not universal precision limits.
