Legend & data sources

Wireframe shows render triangles. Cyan shows native collider outlines; yellow shows joint anchors and body connections. White marks the selected body/joint. Floor and grid are visual only; hiding them does not remove collisions. All views share these settings.

Green: linear velocity, 0.25 m per m/s. Blue: angular velocity, 0.15 m per rad/s. Orange: applied external force command, 0.003 m per N. Pink: applied external torque command, 0.025 m per Nm. Arrows are capped at 2 m; zero vectors are hidden. Numeric values are not capped.

Contact gap is current geometry, not supporting foot load or a first-contact event. Motor caps and external commands are not measured solver/muscle forces. Missing load, first-contact history and slip/drift measurements are explicitly unavailable. Target/anchor errors are geometry, not a stress heatmap.

Export help

Mark copies the current state before pausing. An active grab requires safety cancellation, identified in the export. A new marker replaces the old one; reset clears it. Includes build, variant, run, step, state, cameras, inspection and your note. Local download only; no upload, full input history or replay guarantee.

Camera

Right drag rotates perspective or pans orthographic views. Shift + right or middle drag pans. Wheel zooms; two fingers pan/zoom. One finger grabs unless Camera or Inspect is enabled. Tab and Enter select a viewport. Cameras retain their state across layout changes and resets; view actions are locked during gestures.

Help & safety

Assisted gameplay prototype. Known foot drift; full acceptance pending. Not a Standing result.

Pause preserves state. Step advances one native step. Grabbing, strong pushes, Safety, Escape and focus loss turn assistance off until reset. Inspect is read-only. Free run has no time limit; the optional timer pauses once and allows resume. No automatic recovery.
