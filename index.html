🎯 Goals

Choose a tool, then click the Goblin or arena.

Core Gameplay

Hand: grab and drag a body part; release to throw. Punch/Hammer: apply an impulse. Broom: sweep the legs. Fan/Magnet: apply a continuous force. Throwable objects use physics.

About the ragdoll

The Goblin consists of jointed Rapier rigid bodies. Hits move individual body parts; strong impulses affect the whole body.
