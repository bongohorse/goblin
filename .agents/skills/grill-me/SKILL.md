# Grill Me - Goblin
Use only when unresolved choices materially change implementation.

Ask one small round at a time. Prefer concrete alternatives grounded in the game.

Prioritise:
- player-visible behaviour
- feel and physics
- touch interaction
- success/failure conditions
- visual hierarchy
- persistence/progression
- trade-offs

Do not ask for details discoverable from the repo. When clear, write the decision back into the issue/spec.
